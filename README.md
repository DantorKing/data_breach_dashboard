In the era of information, cybersecurity is as important as ever. Here, we can see the consequences stemming from the lack of its understanding, from the biggest data breaches in the world, the most affected sites and what kind of information attackers are targeting. 

It becomes necessary to study these findings and ensure companies that are recklessly collecting our private data can handle it and hold them accountable when they are unable to.
